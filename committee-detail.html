United Nations General Assembly

Debate international priorities, build consensus across countries, and draft resolutions addressing shared global challenges.

About the Committee

The General Assembly provides a forum for member states to discuss international issues and express collective priorities.

What to Expect

Delegates research their assigned country’s position, deliver speeches, negotiate with other delegations, and work together on draft resolutions.

United Nations Security Council

Examine questions of international peace and security through urgent debate, negotiation, and strategic decision-making.

About the Committee

The Security Council focuses on threats to international peace and security and the diplomatic tools used to address them.

What to Expect

Participants evaluate a crisis, negotiate possible responses, and develop proposals while considering national interests and international law.

Organisation of Islamic Cooperation

Explore cooperation among member states and develop diplomatic responses to issues affecting communities across the world.

About the Committee

The OIC offers a platform for cooperation among its member states on shared political, social, humanitarian, and development concerns.

What to Expect

Delegates represent national perspectives, build coalitions, and propose practical collaborative initiatives.

Human Rights Council

Discuss the protection of fundamental freedoms, humanitarian concerns, and practical ways to strengthen human rights.

About the Committee

The Human Rights Council addresses human-rights situations and considers ways to protect fundamental freedoms and dignity.

What to Expect

Participants assess evidence, discuss international standards, and negotiate recommendations that balance urgency with feasibility.

World Health Organization

Consider global public-health challenges and negotiate cooperative policies that improve health, access, and preparedness.

About the Committee

The World Health Organization coordinates international public-health efforts and supports cooperation on shared health challenges.

What to Expect

Delegates examine public-health priorities, compare policy approaches, and propose coordinated solutions.

International Press Corps

Report on committee proceedings, interview participants, and tell the story of GDF through responsible media coverage.

About the Committee

The International Press Corps documents summit developments and helps participants and audiences understand the debates taking place.

What to Expect

Press members may write reports, conduct interviews, capture approved photography, and communicate events accurately and fairly.
