About GDF

Overview

The Gaziantep Diplomacy Forum (GDF'27) is a student-led international conference designed to bring young people together through diplomacy, dialogue, and collaborative problem-solving. Participants take on the perspectives of different countries, discuss real-world challenges, negotiate with one another, and work toward thoughtful resolutions. Guided by the spirit of “Speak. Represent. Resolve.”, GDF aims to create a space where young voices can be heard and leadership can grow through practice.

Behind the Forum

Why GDF?

Young people are often asked to prepare for the future without being given a place to practise for it. GDF offers that place. By stepping into the role of a diplomat, delegates learn to research a topic in depth, speak with confidence, listen to opposing views, and build agreement across differences. Beyond the debate itself, GDF brings together students from different backgrounds in Gaziantep, turning a few days of committee work into lasting friendships, intercultural understanding, and the confidence to lead.

Vision, Mission and Core Aims

Vision

A generation of young people who approach the world’s challenges with dialogue, empathy, and informed leadership, and who see diplomacy as a path to mutual understanding and peace.

Mission

To provide a well-organised, student-led conference in Gaziantep where participants can research, debate, and negotiate as representatives of different countries, and grow through hands-on diplomatic experience.

Core Aims

  • Develop public speaking, research, and negotiation skills.
  • Encourage dialogue, respect, and understanding between cultures.
  • Raise awareness of pressing international issues.
  • Give young voices a platform to be heard and to lead.
GDF'27

GDF'27 will take place in Gaziantep, bringing together future leaders for an unforgettable experience. Guided by the spirit of “Speak. Represent. Resolve.”, it creates a space where young voices can be heard and leadership can grow through practice.
