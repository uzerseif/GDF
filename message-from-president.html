ÖMER BALUTSH President of GDF'27

On behalf of our Secretariat, it is my great pleasure to welcome you to GDF’27, the Gaziantep Diplomacy Forum, Türkiye.

At GDF’27, delegates will engage with pressing international issues, practice diplomacy, and collaborate with peers from diverse backgrounds. They will be challenged to navigate complex geopolitical, economic, and humanitarian issues while upholding the values of diplomacy, mutual understanding, and international cooperation. Through research, negotiation, and constructive debate, delegates will work toward developing comprehensive policy frameworks and innovative solutions to the challenges facing our world today.

However, GDF extends beyond the committee experience. It offers delegates the opportunity to become part of a diverse international community, build meaningful friendships, exchange cultural perspectives, and create memories that will last long after the summit concludes. Our Secretariat is committed to providing an engaging and enriching experience for every participant, fostering an environment where young people can learn from one another, challenge their ideas, and grow as future leaders.

Dear Friends,

To join GDF'27, you can submit an application within our registration timeline. Before registering, I strongly encourage perusing our website to learn more about the conference. This website contains information about the Secretariat, registration deadlines and fees, conference policies, and committees. If you have any other questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to our team at info@GDF.org.

The Secretariat and I are incredibly excited to welcome you all to GDF'27 in Gaziantep this coming June!
Sincerely,
